## Free Exercise DB

Exercise photo sequences in the named exercise folders are from [Free Exercise DB](https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db), maintained by Yuhonas. The repository identifies the dataset and its exercise images as public domain under the [Unlicense](https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db/blob/main/LICENSE.md). Images are included in Vinland from the repository's `exercises/` directory.
